How to install

On Android

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or the Files app.
  3. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser or the Files app, then tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect asks, tap More details, then Install anyway.

A newer version installs over the old one, and your layouts and settings stay.

On Windows

  1. Click Download for Windows.
  2. Open the installer. It needs no administrator rights.
  3. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

A newer version installs over the old one, and your layouts and settings stay.
