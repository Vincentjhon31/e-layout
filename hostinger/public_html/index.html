Put your office's layout on every photo at once

Pick the PIO footer or your office's own layout, add the photos from an event, and save them all at full size. e-Layout is the new name of PIO Photo Layout.

Download for Android Android phones and tablets Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11 Use it in your browser Nothing to install

Free · Works without internet · Your photos stay on your device

How it works

  1. Pick a layout

    The PIO footer comes with the app. An office with its own layout picks that one, or makes one in the editor.

  2. Add the photos

    Choose a whole event's photos at once. Each one shows up with the layout on it.

  3. Save them

    Every photo keeps its full size and its name, plus _PIO. On a phone they go to Photos, on a computer into a folder you choose, and in the browser into one ZIP file.

Works without internet

Adding photos, editing layouts and saving photos never need a connection. Only downloading the app, checking for a new version and the free font library do.

Your photos stay on your device

e-Layout never uploads your photos. Everything happens on your phone or computer, or in your browser. Saved copies leave out the camera's hidden details, such as the GPS location.

Every office can have its own layout

MDRRMO, the Sangguniang Bayan or any other office can make its layout in the editor: the office name, its seal and logos, contact lines, a header or a footer band. Share the layout as a file, so everyone in the office uses the same one.

  • Text in free fonts, or font files the office already uses
  • Shapes, bands, and icons for phone, email, Facebook and location
  • Logos from PNG files, with the white background taken out when needed
  • Groups, so a logo and its caption move and resize together
  • Pieces stay at the top or bottom edge, so one layout fits wide and tall photos

Good to know

Which phones and computers?
Android 7 or newer, Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), and any up-to-date browser, including on an iPhone or a Mac.
Is it free?
Yes. e-Layout is made by the Municipality of Bongabong.
Does it need the internet?
Not to add, edit or save photos. Only downloading, checking for a new version and the free font library do.
I made layouts in the old browser version.
They are still there. Open the classic version in the same browser, export each layout as a file, then import the file in e-Layout.
How do I update?
The app tells you when a new version is ready and installs it for you. Your layouts and settings stay.

Contact the PIO

Questions, or a layout for your office? Message the Bongabong Public Information Office on Facebook, or visit the Public Information Office at the Office of the Municipal Mayor, Bongabong, Oriental Mindoro.
