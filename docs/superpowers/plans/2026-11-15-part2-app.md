**Goal:** Turn the finished engine (`lib/core`, see `2026-11-14-flutter-mobile-app.md` in this folder) into a usable Android app with a simple, clean iOS-style UI: put a layout on a batch of photos and save them at full size, manage layouts, edit layouts by touch, add fonts, and produce an installable APK.

## Design

**Look.** Light and dark, following the phone. Calm, flat, no blur effects (blur is slow on budget phones), hairline separators, generous spacing, corner radius 12 on cards and 14 on sheets, buttons 50 pt tall with radius 12.

| Token | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| page background | `#F2F2F7` | `#000000` |
| card / sheet | `#FFFFFF` | `#1C1C1E` |
| raised control (fields, tiles) | `#EFEFF4` | `#2C2C2E` |
| text | `#111418` | `#FFFFFF` |
| secondary text | `#5F6670` | `#A1A1AA` |
| separator | `#D9DCE1` | `#38383A` |
| accent (PIO blue) | `#1A6FB3` | `#5CB8FF` |
| destructive | `#C4291C` | `#FF6B5E` |
| warning note background / text | `#FFF4D6` / `#6B4E00` | `#3A2F0B` / `#FFD970` |

**Type.** Bundle Inter (weights 400, 500, 600, 700) as the UI font so Android and Windows look the same. Sizes: large title 32/700, title 20/600, body 17/400, button 17/600, caption 13/400, numbers in tabular figures.

**Icons.** `CupertinoIcons` only.

**Screens.**

1. **Home.** Large title "Photo Layout", a "..." button at the right (About, fonts). Section "Layout": a horizontally scrolling row of cards 132 wide; each shows the layout drawn on the sample scene, its name, and "Office layout" or "On this phone"; the selected card has a 2.5 pt accent ring and a check badge; the last card is a dashed "+" card that offers "New layout", "Import a layout file", "Use a picture". Long-press a card, or tap the selected card's "..." badge, for its actions. Under the row: warning notes. Section "Photos" with the count and a "Clear" text button: a 3-column grid of previews drawn live with `paintComposite`, each with a small round remove button; the first cell is always an "Add" cell. With no photos: one large rounded panel with a photo icon, "Tap to add photos", and "Every photo keeps its full resolution.". A bottom bar pinned above the safe area: primary button "Save 12 photos" and a square "Options" button; while saving it becomes a progress bar with "Saving 3 of 12", the file name, and "Cancel"; afterwards a line such as "Saved 12 photos to Photos, album PIO Photo Layout (48.2 MB)." with a "Share" button.
2. **Options sheet.** Format (JPG, PNG "larger files"), Quality 70 to 100 (hidden for PNG), "Add to file names" text field (default `_PIO`).
3. **Viewer.** Black background, the photo with its layout, swipe sideways between photos, pinch and double-tap to zoom, top bar with the file name and "3 of 12", bottom line "Saves as IMG_1_PIO.jpg, 4000 x 3000 px", buttons "Save this photo" and "Share". Tap toggles the bars.
4. **Editor.** Full screen. Top bar: "Done", the layout name (tap to rename), undo, redo, "..." (Preview on, Layout settings, Save layout as PNG). The canvas fills the middle on a neutral background with a small segmented control "Landscape / Portrait / Square" floating at its top. One-finger drag on a piece moves it; drag a corner handle to resize (proportions kept for text, pictures and icons); drag the round handle below the box to rotate (snaps every 15 degrees, with a light haptic); two fingers anywhere pan and zoom the canvas; tap empty space to deselect; double-tap a text to edit it. Snapping to the photo's edges, centre and other pieces draws thin accent guide lines. At the bottom, a strip of labelled icon tiles 64 wide, scrolling sideways. Nothing selected: Text, Shape, Icon, Picture, Layers, Layout. A piece selected: the tools for its type first (text: Edit, Font, Colour, Style, Outline; shape: Shape, Fill, Border; picture: Replace, Fit, See-through, Border; icon: Icon, Colour, Badge), then Shadow, Opacity, Position, Effects, Layers, Duplicate, Delete. Tapping a tool swaps the strip for a panel about 280 pt tall with a title and "Done"; the canvas stays visible and updates live. Several pieces selected (from Layers, or long-press a piece then tap others): Group or Ungroup, Align, Space evenly, Duplicate, Delete.
5. **Layers sheet.** Topmost piece first. Each row: drag handle, type icon, name (tap to select, "Rename" by swipe or long-press), eye, lock. Groups are a row with their pieces indented. "Select" turns on multi-select with Group and Delete.
6. **Fonts.** Three tabs, "Free library", "From a file", "Your fonts", with the web app's wording.
